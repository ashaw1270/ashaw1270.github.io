- Designed and ran a study of gauge-equivariant neural preconditioners for solving the Dirac equation in lattice QCD; evaluated different model architectures and loss functions with the goal of reducing iterations to convergence on conjugate gradient.
- Delivered a model that cut solver iterations roughly 2x below the state-of-the-art method while reducing its per-system setup cost.
- Proposed three novel architectures and diagnosed why they failed to improve results, tracing the outcome to specific structural properties and narrowing where future gains are likely to be found.
- First-authored the resulting paper with collaborators at MIT and Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory; presented findings in a poster and a paper (soon to be submitted to a workshop).
